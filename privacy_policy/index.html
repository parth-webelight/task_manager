ℹ️ 1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Task Manager mobile application ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to maintaining the trust and confidence of our users by providing a secure task tracking and productivity environment.

By downloading, installing, or using the Task Manager app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our application.

📋 2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide you with seamless task management, cloud synchronization, and task reminder features:

  • Account Information: When you sign up or log in via Firebase Authentication, we collect basic details such as your Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture URL.
  • Task Content & Data: Tasks, subtasks, task categories, due dates, priority levels, and completion status created by you are stored securely in local device storage (SQLite) and synced to Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • User Preferences: App settings such as active theme selection (Classic Indigo, Emerald Green, Sunset Orange), selected locale/language (English, Hindi, Gujarati), and notification settings.
  • Technical & Diagnostics Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic info, such as device model, OS version, and crash logs to troubleshoot errors and maintain app stability.

🔐 3. Device Permissions & Usage

To deliver core capabilities, Task Manager requests explicit runtime permissions on your device. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time via your device's System Settings:

🔔 Notifications

Used strictly to trigger timely alarm notifications and reminders for your scheduled tasks.

🎤 Microphone & Audio

Used when you interact with Speech-to-Text feature for AI voice task creation. Audio is processed on-device/system API.

🖼️ Camera & Storage

Used exclusively if you choose to select or capture a profile photo or attach images to tasks.

⏰ Alarms & Reminders

Required on Android 12+ (USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) to ensure precision for task due reminders.

⚙️ 4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to power and enhance your task management experience. Specifically:

  • Core Functionality: Storing, editing, filtering, and organizing your daily tasks, projects, and reminders.
  • Cloud Synchronization: Enabling multi-device sync and backup via secure Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Smart Reminders: Triggering exact local notifications before task due dates.
  • Personalization: Remembering your custom theme settings, category colors, and language preferences.
  • Security & Authentication: Verifying user identity and protecting against unauthorized access.

🔒 We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or task details to any third party or advertisers.

🛡️ 6. Security & Data Retention

We prioritize the security of your data. We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and secure database storage mechanisms locally and in the cloud.

Retention: Your data remains stored for as long as your account remains active. Local data is retained until you uninstall the application or clear app storage. Cloud data is retained until you delete your tasks or request account deletion.

👤 7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Access & Modify: You can edit or delete any task, subtask, category, or profile detail directly within the app at any time.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can grant or revoke notification, camera, or microphone permissions at any time through your Android OS settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud/local task records, you can submit a deletion request from the Profile screen or contact us at the support email below. All data will be permanently wiped within 30 days.

👶 8. Children's Privacy

Task Manager is designed for general productivity and is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to remove such data from our servers.
